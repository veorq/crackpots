# Crackpots and charlatans 

> It is impossible for someone to lie unless he thinks he knows the
truth. Producing bullshit requires no such conviction. (H. Frankfurt) 

> If you see fraud and dont say 'fraud', you're fraud. (N. Taleb)


This page lists individuals who exploit their academic
credentials (if any), or former legitimate work (if any), to spread
pseudoscience, speculative hype, or outright nonsense for personal gain.
We focus on individuals affiliated with reputable institutions who have
drifted into profit- and attention-driven sensationalism, publishing
pop-science books or blog posts, appearing on TED stages or podcasts, and
presenting exaggeration or fiction as fact.

These figures trade on their perceived authority, misleading the public
and undermining real scientific discourse.

## Chief offenders 

* [Robert E. Grant](https://robertedwardgrant.com/) — Time AI crypto snake oil, pseudomathematics, "theory of everything", and more

* [Michio Kaku](https://mkaku.org/), CUNY — quantum physics and quantum computing sensationalism

* [Ray Kurzweil](https://www.thekurzweillibrary.com/) — singularity futurism, digital immortality grift

* [Avi Loeb](https://lweb.cfa.harvard.edu/~loeb/), Harvard — aliens existence sensationalism

### Disclaimers

* This list excludes obvious frauds such as flat-earthers, medbed enthusiasts, or astrologers.

* It also omits crackpots in the fields of climate denial and antivax
  disinformation, for reasons of scope and sanity.

* Not all entries are equally culpable. Degrees and types of crackpotism vary.

* Inclusion requires both significant visibility and repeated offenses
  against intellectual honesty.

* Yes, some listed individuals have contributed legitimate work. This
  does not exempt them from scrutiny once they begin misrepresenting
  science for attention or profit.

* The list is not exhaustive.
